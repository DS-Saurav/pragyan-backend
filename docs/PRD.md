## B. Product vision and context

**Documented purpose** (from `PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`): the system manages the day-to-day operations of Pragyan Pathashala school and serves its public website.

- **Management side:** a web application for administrators, principals, academic coordinators, teachers, librarians, and accountants covering admissions, students, staffing, timetables, attendance, examinations, fees and accounting, payroll, library, inventory and school assets, helpdesk and facilities support, personnel documents, student conduct, communications, and internal settings/dashboards.
- **Public side:** a hosted public website for notices, team information, the school calendar, and general school information for prospective parents and the community. Recorded owner direction (M-04, 2026-10-26) frames this as a dynamic school marketing/communications platform with backend-managed school images, configurable rotating hero imagery, news, categorized galleries, contact/team information, and future student blogging/school-magazine capability — most of that direction is **not yet implemented**.

No executive, user-demographic, or strategic context beyond the recorded owner decisions was available when drafting 0.1/0.2. Direction on goals and first-release scope was received on 2026-10-26 (M-01/M-02); the framing below is consistent with that direction but has not been reviewed line-by-line with the owner and remains subject to final review.

> **Product framing (aligned with recorded owner direction M-01/M-02; subject to final review):** "A single integrated system in which one record per student flows from first admission enquiry through academic and financial life-cycle events, with a trustworthy public information site." This framing reflects the owner's recorded goals — central school administration and recordkeeping, teachers maintaining academic records and completing/printing results and marksheets, and parents receiving notifications and checking student status and results online. Its line-by-line ratification remains final-review work.

## C. Users and stakeholders

### C.1 Implemented application roles (verified in `config/permissions.php`)

Access to management modules is controlled by a central role→permission map (10 roles, 12 permission areas). Module access is defined by **role membership only**. An admin bypass grants users holding the `admin` role access to all permission-gated modules.

| Role | Intended usage (as documented) |
|---|---|
| Admin | Full access (explicit `Gate::before` bypass) |
| Principal | Academic oversight, discipline, staff, payroll visibility |
| Coordinator | Academic operations, attendance, timetable, examinations |
| Teacher | Class academic work, attendance, marks entry, lesson planning |
| Librarian | Library issue/return management |
| Accountant | Fees, accounting, receipts, payroll operations |
| Staff | Cross-cutting staff-facing resources |
| Student | Personal academic data, hall tickets |
| Parent | Child data (currently schema-segregated; no active linking), fee view/receipt |
| Superadmin | Administrative oversight actions |

**Important caveat:** two role systems coexist. Module gating reads the relational `user_roles`/`role` tables, while several staff-selection interfaces still read the legacy `users.role` string column; `HelpdeskController::staff()` remains on the legacy source (verified). Owner direction recorded under M-05 is centralized administrator-controlled permissions, granting/revoking access by feature and module, with parents seeing only their own linked children's authorized records and students seeing only their own authorized information. Retiring the legacy `users.role` column remains a technical follow-up (L-02), and child/parent account linking is not implemented (FR-090).

### C.2 Broader stakeholder groups (documented, but *not* all implemented)

Students, parents/guardians, teachers, administrators, librarians, accountants, prospective parents, and the school community are documented as system audiences. **Not all of them have working end-to-end workflows today**, e.g. student/parent records are not linked to login accounts (linkage direction was recorded under M-10 on 2026-10-26, but the implementation does not exist — see FR-090). Unimplemented workflows must not be assumed.

## E. Product scope

### E.1 Existing capability scope — documented today

**Management:** Authentication and role/permission control (`Breeze` login/logout/registration/password reset/profile/actions); admission pipeline (registered→admitted/rejected with input validation) and student record creation; class/subject/teacher assignment management; daily class attendance and staff check-in; timetable grid and period slots; lesson planning; fac/staff induction records; examination setup, schedules, hall tickets (PDF), marks entry, marks lock, published results, marksheet PDFs; fee heads/structures, requisitions, invoices, payments, receipts, refunds, bill reconciliation; payroll configuration and full generation cycle; base accounting transactions, cash/bank ledgers, CSV exports; library catalogue/issue/return/damage flows; inventory and asset tracking; helpdesk tickets, complaints, inquiries, documents, teacher conduct tracking for administrators; settings, notice dispatch, and role dashboards.

**Public:** notices, team listing, calendar page, and general school info, served from Blade templates using a shared `layouts.public` layout (see Section I).

### E.2 Partially implemented or unverified capabilities

- Bulk student promotion exists as logic with no wired route (L-09 above).
- `dompdf` is in `composer.json` but unlinked; existing PDF generation comes from plain browser/print rendering plus custom PDF controllers (see `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`).
- Correctness of generated marksheets and hall tickets (attendance thresholds, computed grades) depends on unverified SQL and unexecuted tests (L-11, L-12, L-05).
- Server deployment pipeline and production data-store engine are not established in-repo (M-06).
- Public site breadth: grew to a 9-content-page set during the documentation window; owner direction recorded under M-04 (2026-10-26) confirms a dynamic marketing/communications platform whose remaining scope is listed in E.4.

### E.3 Out of scope (unless the owner changes it)

- Native mobile apps; standalone PWA distribution. (Recorded owner direction M-08: a responsive, mobile-friendly web application for parents, teachers, and administration on phones and desktops; no separate native mobile app requirement has been approved.)
- Online public job posting, e-commerce website functions, online public fee payment。
- Live chat avatars or unsupported arbitrary integrations from external vendors without owner agreement.
- Nothing here represents a claim that a feature is impossible — it just has not been scoped, budgeted, or approved. New capabilities enter through Section M.

### E.4 First-release scope and sequencing (owner direction recorded 2026-10-26)

**Scope — seven areas (M-02):** the owner requires the first complete release to cover seven areas: administration/planning; teacher records/examinations; the parent portal and notifications; the public website; library/inventory; attendance/timetable; and fees/accounting.

**Sequencing and risk priority (M-03/M-09):** the owner prioritizes a school-authentic marksheet, the public marketing website, and library management, with other modules improved gradually. Work order (revised by the owner during planning on 2026-10-26; the earlier security-first M-09 order remains in the revision history and is superseded): first, planned product features and required functional workflows are completed — the marksheet, public website, and library first, other modules gradually; then existing security-audit findings, bugs, and data inconsistencies are remediated; then data-loss risks are resolved and academic/financial record reliability is ensured; finally, fixes are verified and the completed workflows tested before any production release. After an admission is approved and the required process completed, the student and classroom records should be created/linked automatically, reusing the parent/guardian information entered at admission and avoiding duplicate records — this automation is **not implemented today** (see FR-010/FR-011).

**Public website (M-04):** a dynamic school marketing/communications platform with backend-managed school images, configurable rotating hero imagery, specific academic and extracurricular information, news and future student blogging/school-magazine capability, categorized galleries, and contact/team information; administrators control content and may delegate publishing/editing authority. Rotating hero imagery, backend image management, and blogging/magazine capability are **not implemented today**; student assignment tracking also remains a planned capability outside the recordings above.

## J. Data, security, and technical constraints

Detailed architecture, schema, routing, and security analysis live in `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `MODELS_AND_DATABASE.md`, `ROUTES_AND_ENDPOINTS.md`, and `AUTHENTICATION_AND_AUTHORIZATION.md`. PRD-relevant top-line constraints are:

1. **Authorization correctness** is central (NFR-01/02). Known authorization-adjacent risks: dual role sources (L-02), About page lacking middleware (L-07 — 0.4/D1 re-verified and classified *missing authorization*; scheduled for Stage D1.5), role-less accounts passing staff middleware (L-08), and notice store/destroy lacking permission-level authorization (0.4/D1 finding; D1.5). These are risks, not accepted behavior.
2. **Data integrity** is enforced transactionally where done, but several audit-risk records rely on conventions (write-on-read fixtures: timetable create-period, download counters — L-01), and MySQL-specific SQL limits portability (L-05).
3. **Testing reality:** 40 test files exist (2026-11-01 inventory); the suite was executed once by U1.1 (289 tests / 1,426 assertions passing — recorded baseline), but nothing in this PRD should be treated as "tested" beyond that run, and D-series feature work must report against the baseline (L-12 context; P-0.1/§L). Running and extending the suite remains first-release work the owner should prioritize (M-09).
4. **Production decisions partially answered, technically open:** owner direction (M-06, recorded 2026-10-26) requires online server availability, secure access, regular backups, and a recovery process; the hosting provider, production DB engine, backup frequency, and recovery targets remain technical decisions and are not established in-repo; assuming MySQL production is unconfirmed.
5. **Schema safety:** intentionally added guard patterns (unique compound keys, mass-assignment guards, pivot constraints) are in place; a column-drift risk in results output remains (L-04).
